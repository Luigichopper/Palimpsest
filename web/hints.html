P0.0: Seed Steganography

Rung 1: Gentle Nudge

The image holds more than pixels and text. Standard graphical noise can conceal data at the bit layer.

Rung 2: Method

Inspect the least significant bit (LSB) of each red color channel. The payload begins with magic header 'PLMP' (4 bytes) followed by 2 bytes representing length.

Rung 3: Near-Answer

Use Python with PIL: iterate pixels (x, y), extract pixel[0] & 1, accumulate bits into bytes until the header 'PLMP' and length are read.

P0.3: L1 Bunker Door Pottery Code

Rung 1: Gentle Nudge

Examine the decorated pots on pedestals outside the bunker entrance, and read sherd_legend.txt in Odette's home directory.

Rung 2: Method

P0.4: Annex Frame Room

Rung 1: Gentle Nudge

Item frames in Minecraft click through 8 discrete rotational steps (0 to 7).

Rung 2: Method

The rotations of the Compass, Clock, and Echo Shard form a 3-digit octal number. Convert octal to decimal.

Rung 3: Near-Answer

Frame rotations are 5, 2, 7 -> octal 527. (5 × 64) + (2 × 8) + 7 = 343.
